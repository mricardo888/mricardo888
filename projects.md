<a id="salus"></a>
## 🛡️ Salus: Privacy-First Coordination of Benefits Engine
**🏆 [UofTHacks 13 Winner: Best Use of MongoDB Atlas] | [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/mricardo888/salus) | [Devpost](https://devpost.com/software/salus-9javs3)**

Salus is a **local-first, universal benefits engine** engineered to solve the complex bureaucracy of medical billing. Designed with a Zero-Trust architecture, it mathematically stacks private insurance with public government aid to eliminate financial toxicity for patients.

* **Zero-Trust Security:** Utilizes **1Password Passkeys** to decrypt a local browser vault containing sensitive PII. Identity data never touches the server.
* **Multi-Agent Reasoning:** Orchestrates 4 specialized AI agents using **LangGraph** (Extractor, Actuary, Social Worker, Coordinator) that analyze hospital bills, private insurance rules, and local government policies.
* **Voice Accessibility:** Integrates **ElevenLabs** for native language text-to-speech, ensuring complex financial advice is accessible to everyone.
* **Tech Stack:** `Next.js` • `TypeScript` • `Python FastAPI` • `Google Gemini 1.5 Pro` • `MongoDB Atlas Vector Search` • `LangGraph` • `1Password Passkeys`

## 🏭 TranSignal: Autonomous Supply Chain Resilience Agent
**🤖 [Hack The Future] | [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/mricardo888/TranSignal)**

TranSignal is an autonomous AI operations co-pilot designed to help mid-market manufacturers proactively predict, mitigate, and respond to global supply chain disruptions before SLA breaches or stockouts occur.

* **Risk Intelligence Engine:** Calculates revenue-at-risk estimation and operational impact modeling using deterministic math against real-time disruption data (e.g., port strikes, weather delays).
* **Gemini 2.5 Flash Core:** Uses GenAI to evaluate multi-dimensional supply chain trade-offs, determining the most cost-effective mitigation path (such as expedited shipping vs. SLA penalties).
* **Operations Control Tower:** A real-time dashboard featuring geospatial mapping, interactive reasoning traces, and clear Human-In-The-Loop approval gates based on automated financial thresholds.
* **Tech Stack:** `Python` • `FastAPI` • `Streamlit` • `Google Gemini 2.5 Flash` • `PyDeck` • `Pandas`

<a id="clutter2cash"></a>
## 🧺 Clutter2Cash: Sustainability & Resale AI
**♻️ [MLH Hackathon Project] | [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/mricardo888/Clutter2Cash) | [Devpost](https://devpost.com/software/clutter2cash)**

Clutter2Cash is an intelligent mobile application that helps users discover value in unused household goods. By analyzing a photo of an item, the app automatically identifies it, estimates fair resale value, and calculates the carbon emissions saved by keeping it out of landfills.

* **Multimodal AI Vision:** Leverages **Google Gemini API** to instantly categorize items, estimate market pricing across second-hand platforms, and calculate CO₂ offsets.
* **Seamless Authentication:** Secure, passwordless user authentication and profile management powered by **Auth0**.
* **Cross-Platform Mobile:** Built with React Native and Expo for a smooth, native iOS and Android experience.
* **Tech Stack:** `React Native (Expo)` • `TypeScript` • `Node.js` • `Express.js` • `MongoDB` • `Google Gemini API` • `Auth0`
